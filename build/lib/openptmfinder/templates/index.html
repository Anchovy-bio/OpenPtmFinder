Volcano Plot

Volcano plot for the selected modification type.

The bar chart shows the landscape of identified modifications for a specific protein, as well as the peptide coverage of the protein. The y-axis represents the number of spectra confirming the found modification. The x-axis represents the peptide sequence. Color is used to indicate the peptide coverage of the protein.

The pie chart illustrates the distribution of all identified modification types across the entire dataset.
